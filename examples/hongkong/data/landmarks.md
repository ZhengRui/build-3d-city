# Hong Kong landmarks: research notes

## M4 figures (landmarks the building table can't draw; `scripts/m4l_structures.py`)

Photo research of 6 Oct 2026 (worker r-hk-m4l: Wikimedia Commons searches, contact sheets 4 wide, PIL samples in
boxes checked by eye; Wikipedia, CTBUH and architects' pages for published figures). (src) = a published figure,
(samp) = sampled from a photo, (est.) = read off a photo or derived. Plans and heights of the rows come from LandsD's
Building layer (`landsd_prepared/landsd_E600_ground.gpkg`, by BuildingCSUID); the rows a figure replaces are excluded
in city.toml (`[buildings] exclude.buildingcsuid`, the list the script prints), the rows a figure stands on keep a
`fix_h`. Every figure's 0 is an absolute height (06_tiles' base under the row plus its height), so a mast stands on its
roof whatever the ground under its point. Figures are `[[structures]]` in `generated/structures-m4l.toml`; IFC and
ICC are profiles in `landmark_facades.csv`. The engine's figure gained `wedges` (a prism with its own top height at each
outline point: sloped and curved roofs), an opt-in key no other city uses.
